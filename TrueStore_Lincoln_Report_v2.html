5 Questions to Ask Before You Sign

The lease is where storage facilities make their money. Here's how to protect yourself.

📅
"What's my rate in month 4?"
The most important question. If the answer differs from what's advertised, that's your red flag. Get the ongoing rate in writing before you sign.
📈
"How often do you raise rates?"
Industry average is 8–10% annually. Facilities with "at our discretion" language in the fine print can raise rates anytime with 30 days notice.
🔒
"Is there a price lock?"
U-Haul offers a 1-year price lock as standard — the gold standard in Lincoln. Ask any facility if they'll match it in writing.
💳
"Are there fees beyond rent?"
Watch for: admin/setup fees, mandatory insurance ($15–$20/mo), credit card surcharges (StorageMart charges 2.5%), and required lock purchases.
🤝
"Do you price-match?"
Powerful with local operators especially. Show them a competitor's rate — many will match or beat it rather than lose the rental.

How We Score

TrueStore's transparency score (1–10) measures how clearly a facility discloses real ongoing pricing before a consumer commits. We evaluate: whether ongoing rates are published online, whether rate-increase language appears in fine print, whether fees are disclosed upfront, and whether a price lock exists.

A score of 7–8 means a consumer can make a fully informed decision from the website alone. A score of 4–6 means pricing exists and is accessible in a browser, but requires extra steps to confirm the ongoing rate. A score of 3 means pricing is shown but fine print allows rates to change "at any time at our discretion."

The "Local & Call-First" category is not scored — these are verified independently owned operators who prefer phone inquiries over web listings. They are not penalized for this. A family business that has served Lincoln for 35 years and works with customers directly is not comparable to a corporate chain with deceptive fine print, and it would be misleading to rank them the same way.
